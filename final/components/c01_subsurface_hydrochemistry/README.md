## 🔬 Core Scientific Scope
1. **Smackover Oolitic Carbonate Hydrochemistry:** Detailed analysis of high-salinity brine speciation ($Na^+$, $Ca^{2+}$, $Mg^{2+}$, $Sr^{2+}$, $Cl^-$).
2. **Selective Adsorption Kinetics:** Layered Double Hydroxide (LDH) $\text{LiCl}\cdot 2\text{Al(OH)}_3$ crystal lattice kinetics under high enthalpy.
3. **Silica & Strontium Sulfate Scaling Mitigation:** Thermodynamic saturation modeling preventing premature fouling of surface extraction beds.
