## Abstract
Under the United States Inflation Reduction Act (IRA Section 45X and Section 30D), domestic critical mineral producers qualify for Tier-1 tax credits only if their extraction lifecycle adheres to strict greenhouse gas intensity caps. In Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) and geothermal co-production from repurposed wellbores, operators must empirically prove that producing millions of barrels of deep hydrothermal brine does not release unmitigated fugitive methane ($\text{CH}_4$) through compromised casing seals. This paper introduces a multiscale verification framework coupling top-of-atmosphere trace gas column retrievals from spaceborne spectrometers (Sentinel-5P TROPOMI and NASA TEMPO) with continuous downhole fiber-optic Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and surface edge telemetry. By assimilating atmospheric plume dispersion inversions with acoustic fluid transit models, our system generates tamper-evident proof of zero-emissions wellbore integrity, satisfying the empirical mandates of EPA Subpart W.

**Keywords:** Remote Sensing, TROPOMI, NASA TEMPO, Distributed Acoustic Sensing, Methane Emissions, IRA Section 45X.

## 1. Top-Down Satellite Inversion & Bottom-Up Acoustics
The framework assimilates two independent observation tiers:
1. **Orbital Tier:** Retrieves daily dry-air mole fraction methane columns ($\text{XCH}_4$, ppb) at $5.5 \times 3.5\text{ km}^2$ resolution.
2. **Subsurface Fiber Tier:** Interrogates single-mode optical fibers clamped behind casing, analyzing strain acoustic signatures at $1.45\text{ kHz}$ to detect micro-annular gas leaks below $0.01\text{ kg/hr}$.
