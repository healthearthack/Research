## 🔬 Core Scientific Scope
1. **Spaceborne Column Retrievals:** Processing Level-2 total column methane ($\text{XCH}_4$) from the Sentinel-5P TROPOMI spectrometer and NASA TEMPO.
2. **Ground-Truth Acoustic Inversion:** Bottom-of-well Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) measuring micro-annular fluid bubbling at $1.45\text{ kHz}$.
3. **Statutory Compliance:** Fulfilling the mandatory empirical measurement protocols under **EPA GHGRP Subpart W** and defending zero-fee status under **Clean Air Act § 136**.
