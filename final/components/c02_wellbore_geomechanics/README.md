## 🔬 Core Scientific Scope
1. **Thermo-Mechanical Casing Fatigue:** Modeling cyclic thermal contraction and expansion as $140^\circ\text{C}$ brine is produced and reinjected.
2. **Cement Sheath Integrity:** Finite element evaluation of micro-annular channeling under high hydrostatic overburdens.
3. **Plugging & Abandonment (P&A) Liability Conversion:** Converting idle liabilities into income-generating hydrothermal assets.
