## 🔬 Core Scientific & Socio-Technical Scope
1. **Transatlantic Clean Energy Diplomacy:** Connecting U.S. clean tech innovation (IRA tax credits, Smackover critical minerals) with UAE / Middle Eastern energy transition hubs (ADEK, Masdar City, Heriot-Watt Dubai).
2. **Caregivers in Science Equity Charter:** Institutional framework addressing non-traditional researcher paths, anchored to the Heriot-Watt Athena SWAN Carer Support Fund and NSF Career-Life Balance initiatives.
3. **De-Stigmatization & Inclusive Excellence:** Establishing formal institutional support mechanisms for researchers balancing elder care, family responsibilities, and advanced doctoral scholarship.
