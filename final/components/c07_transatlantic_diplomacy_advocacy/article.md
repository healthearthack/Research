## Abstract
The rapid global transition to decarbonized energy systems requires not only technical breakthroughs in mineral extraction and cybersecurity, but also resilient international diplomatic corridors and equitable academic research structures. This paper articulates a dual socio-technical charter uniting transatlantic clean energy diplomacy with systemic support for non-traditional researchers. Grounded in the author's lineage as a U.S. Department of Defense Project GO Arabic scholar and Air Force ROTC alumnus, we examine the strategic convergence between North American critical mineral independence (U.S. IRA Section 45X) and United Arab Emirates sustainable energy investment (Heriot-Watt Dubai and Masdar City clean-tech initiatives). In parallel, we formalize the **Caregivers in Science Equity Charter**, anchored to the Heriot-Watt Athena SWAN Carer Support Fund and NSF Career-Life Balance provisions. We demonstrate that institutional protections for scholars balancing elder and family healthcare obligations expand doctoral completion rates and enrich engineering innovation with real-world resilience.

**Keywords:** Energy Diplomacy, Transatlantic Clean Tech, Caregivers in Science, Athena SWAN, Heriot-Watt Dubai, Project GO, Critical Minerals.

## 1. The Transatlantic Clean Energy Corridor
The United States and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) nations share complementary strategic priorities in the post-petroleum economy:
* **The U.S. Mandate:** Securing domestic battery-grade lithium supply chains under IRA Section 30D/45X.
* **The UAE Mandate:** Reinvesting sovereign energy capital into global desalination, geothermal co-production, and advanced cyber-physical telemetry.
* **The Academic Axis:** Operating across Heriot-Watt University's Dubai and Edinburgh campuses creates an operational bridge for technology transfer, student mobility, and cross-border standards harmonization.

## 2. Institutional Framework for Caregivers in Science
Doctoral attrition disproportionately impacts researchers with caregiving responsibilities:
* **Athena SWAN Alignment:** Institutional caregiver grants and flexible remote telemetry auditing prevent career interruption during family medical crises.
* **Dual-Track Research Modeling:** By structuring the doctoral thesis as an interlocking component library, researchers can achieve steady, measurable milestone outputs independently of physical laboratory presence.
