## 🛰️ Technical & Scientific Integration: Spaceborne Ground-Truthing
Mature oil and gas wellbores repurposed for Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) and low-enthalpy geothermal energy must maintain flawless environmental containment to qualify for federal clean-energy tax incentives (U.S. IRA Section 45X and 30D). 

This research track builds a coupled telemetry pipeline:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Orbital Remote Sensing: TROPOMI & TEMPO                     │
│ Spaceborne Methane (CH4), VOCs & Atmospheric Boundary Layer │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ Satellite Telemetry Stream
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ High-Resolution Chemical Transport Modeling (CMAQ / WRF)    │
│ The Holloway Group Analytical Engine (UW–Madison)           │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ Ground Validation Correlation
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Ground-Level OT Wellhead Telemetry & DAS Acoustic Strain    │
│ Heriot-Watt University Repurposed Wellbore Network          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Spaceborne Telemetry:** Ingesting orbital observations from **TROPOMI** (TROPOspheric Monitoring Instrument aboard Sentinel-5P) and NASA's **TEMPO** (Tropospheric Emissions: Monitoring of Pollution) to monitor regional greenhouse gas and volatile organic compound plumes over Smackover DLE well fields.
2. **Subsurface Ground-Truthing:** Correlating orbital column density readings with wellbore-level Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and Modbus/DNP3 SCADA pressure sensors.
3. **Zero-Leakage Certification:** Delivering an automated, tamper-proof verification architecture that proves repurposed geothermal and brine extraction wells operate with near-zero fugitive methane emissions.
