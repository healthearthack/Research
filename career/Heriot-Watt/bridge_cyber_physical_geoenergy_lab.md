## 1. The Strategic Opportunity at Heriot-Watt
Heriot-Watt University possesses two world-leading but historically distinct research powerhouses:
1. **Institute of GeoEnergy Engineering (IGE - Edinburgh Campus):** Globally renowned for reservoir modeling, HPHT rock mechanics, core flood analysis, and carbon storage.
2. **School of Mathematical & Computer Sciences (MACS - Dubai & Edinburgh Campuses):** Renowned for software architecture, Internet of Things (IoT), smart grid data platforms, and explainable AI for cybersecurity (led by faculty including **Dr. Christos Chrysoulas**).

**The Bridge Thesis:** The energy transition requires bridging these two disciplines. Subsurface fluid engineering cannot scale without digital twins, real-time closed-loop control, and cyber-hardened operational technology (OT) networks. This bridge creates the **Cyber-Physical Subsurface Telemetry Lab** at Heriot-Watt, establishing a permanent academic nexus between IGE and MACS.

## 3. Postdoctoral & Fellowship Funding Strategy
To transition from doctoral completion (`Energy, EngD`) into permanent research leadership at Heriot-Watt, this track targets three multi-million-pound funding vehicles:

1. **UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship (FLF):**
   * *Theme:* Securing the Critical Mineral Supply Chain via Cyber-Physical Subsurface Telemetry.
   * *Scope:* 4-year funding providing full salary, research staff, and laboratory access across both the Edinburgh and Dubai campuses.
2. **EPSRC Standard Research Grant:**
   * *Theme:* Autonomous Edge Computing for High-Temperature Geothermal Wellhead Management.
3. **Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship:**
   * *Theme:* Next-generation industrial IoT architectures for deep-reservoir energy co-production.
