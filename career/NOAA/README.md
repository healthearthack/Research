1. **Pre-Emptive Grid & Wellhead Hardening:** Developing physical risk models that ingest NOAA HRRR (High-Resolution Rapid Refresh) atmospheric state data to forecast freeze-offs, extreme convective wind shears, and heat-wave efficiency degradation 24 to 72 hours ahead of impact.
2. **Atmospheric-Subsurface Boundary Coupling:** Investigating how atmospheric pressure variations, solar irradiance extremes, and boundary layer inversions impact surface heat exchange rates in closed-loop geothermal cycles.
3. **Federal Operational Impact:** Translating raw academic modeling into hardened, operational guidance for the federal Weather-Ready Nation initiative.
