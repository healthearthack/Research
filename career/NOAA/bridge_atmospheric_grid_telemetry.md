**Document Type:** Federal Civil Service & Applied Research Bridge  
**Target Unit:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Weather Service (NWS)  
**Divisions:** Environmental Modeling Center (EMC) & Office of Science and Technology Integration (OSTI)  
**Author:** Andrew C. Kieckhefer (B.A. Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences, UW–Madison; Doctoral Candidate, Heriot-Watt)  
**Core Domain:** Planetary Boundary Layer Forecasting, Weather-Ready Nation & Energy Infrastructure Resilience  

---

## 1. Federal Mission & Operational Urgency
The modern electrical grid and emerging clean energy facilities (geothermal power stations, direct lithium extraction brine facilities, and large-scale industrial batteries) are acutely vulnerable to sudden atmospheric extremes:
* **The Problem:** Severe cold waves (e.g., Winter Storm Uri), sudden convective wind shears, and multi-day heat domes induce catastrophic simultaneous supply loss (wellhead freeze-offs, cooling tower thermodynamic collapse) and peak demand surges.
* **The Opportunity:** Extending NOAA’s state-of-the-art numerical weather prediction (NWP) models directly into industrial operational technology (OT) control systems.

2. **Presidential Management Fellows (PMF) STEM Track:** A two-year premier training and leadership development program designed for terminal degree recipients (Ph.D. / EngD), offering accelerated progression into GS-13 scientific leadership.
