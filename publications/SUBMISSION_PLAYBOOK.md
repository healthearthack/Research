## 🌐 PART 2: The 3 Preprint Platforms (Minting Official DOIs)

To get permanent, global Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) that other researchers cite:

### 1. EarthArXiv (For WP-01 & WP-03)
* **Website:** [eartharxiv.org](https://eartharxiv.org/) (Powered by the California Digital Library / Janeway).
* **Scope:** All Earth, geoscience, critical minerals, and environmental papers.
* **Action:** Click **"Submit a Preprint"**, sign in with your ORCID or email, upload `WP01_DLE_Wellbore_Repurposing_Review.pdf`, and paste the abstract.
* **Outcome:** You receive an immediate citable DOI (e.g., `10.31223/X5...`).

### 2. TechRxiv (For WP-02)
* **Website:** [techrxiv.org](https://www.techrxiv.org/) (Operated by IEEE).
* **Scope:** Electrical engineering, cybersecurity, industrial informatics, and IoT.
* **Action:** Click **"Submit Preprint"**, sign in with your IEEE account or email, upload `WP02_OT_Cybersecurity_SCADA_Telemetry.pdf`.
* **Outcome:** You receive an official IEEE-branded preprint DOI.

### 3. ESS Open Archive (Backup for WP-03)
* **Website:** [essopenarchive.org](https://essopenarchive.org/) (Operated by the American Geophysical Union - AGU).
* **Scope:** Atmospheric science, satellite observations, and environmental policy.

## 👨‍🏫 PART 3: How to Sign Up as an Early-Career Peer Reviewer Tonight

Journals actively look for quantitative reviewers in atmospheric science, fluid dynamics, and industrial systems. Reviewing builds immediate peer credibility and connects you directly to journal editors:

### Platform A: Web of Science Researcher Profile (Clarivate / Publons)
1. Go to [**access.clarivate.com**](https://access.clarivate.com/) and create an account using `weather.amazon.go@gmail.com`.
2. Navigate to **"My Reviewer Profile"**.
3. Toggle **"Interested in reviewing for journals"** to **ON**.
4. Enter your Reviewer Subject Categories:
   * *Atmospheric Science & Remote Sensing*
   * *Energy & Fuels / Geothermal Energy*
   * *Cybersecurity & Industrial Automation*
   * *Petroleum Engineering & Formation Water*
5. Add Institution: `University of Wisconsin–Madison (Alumni)` and `Heriot-Watt University (Applicant/Collaborator)`.

### Platform B: Elsevier Reviewer Hub (For *Applied Energy* & *Desalination*)
1. Go to [**reviewerhub.elsevier.com**](https://reviewerhub.elsevier.com/).
2. Create an account with your scientific credentials.
3. Under **"Reviewing Interests"**, add: `Direct Lithium Extraction`, `Geothermal Energy`, `Wellbore Integrity`, `Industrial IoT`.
4. Editors searching for reviewers on new DLE manuscripts will see your profile and invite you to review.

### Platform C: IOP Publishing Reviewer Community (For *Environmental Research Letters*)
1. Go to [**publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org**](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/).
2. Register as a reviewer for *Environmental Research Letters*.
3. Add specialties: `Satellite Remote Sensing`, `Methane Emissions`, `TROPOMI`, `Energy Policy`.

### Platform D: IEEE Reviewer Pool (For *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics*)
1. Go to [**ieee.org**](https://www.ieee.org/) and create your IEEE Account.
2. Sign into IEEE ScholarOne Manuscripts.
3. Check the box to opt in as a volunteer reviewer for the **IEEE Industrial Electronics Society** and **IEEE Internet of Things Journal**.
