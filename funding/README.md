## 🏥 Human Realities: Caregivers to PhD & Family Hardship Feasibility

Doctoral research is often structured under the unrealistic assumption that researchers have zero family dependencies. This candidacy explicitly plans for the reality of **primary caregiving for elderly parents managing severe cardiovascular conditions**:

1. **Remote Simulation Architecture:**
   * By designing the research workflow around cloud-native digital twins ([`website.oil:8000`](http://localhost:8000) & [`website.h2o:8001`](http://localhost:8001)) and autonomous Python background monitors ([`agent/`](../agent/)), rigorous laboratory-grade research can be executed from a home office while maintaining direct family care.
2. **Heriot-Watt Athena SWAN Carer Support Fund:**
   * Direct institutional bursaries to cover respite care costs during high-intensity academic deadlines, conference travel, and doctoral viva defense.
3. **U.S. Dependent Care & Medicaid Self-Directed Waivers:**
   * Accessing structured self-directed eldercare caregiver stipends (e.g., IRIS / Medicaid Caregiver programs) and the federal National Family Caregiver Support Program (NFCSP) to ensure sustained financial equilibrium throughout the degree tenure.
4. **NSF Career-Life Balance (CLB) Supplements:**
   * Pre-identified federal mechanisms offering up to $30,000 in support for researchers balancing primary dependent care emergencies during active grant execution.
