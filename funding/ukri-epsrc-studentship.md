## 1. Mechanism Overview & Funding Scope
UKRI/EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Awards provide institutional funding block-grants to top UK research universities to support world-leading PhD students in strategic national priority areas:

* **Tuition Fees:** Full waiver covered at the Home/International rate.
* **Annual Stipend:** UKRI standard national minimum stipend (~£19,237 to £20,500/yr tax-free).
* **Consumables & Travel:** Substantial institutional budget for laboratory HPHT core flooding, micro-CT beamtime, and international conference travel.

## 3. Departmental Allocation Pathway
Heriot-Watt University allocates EPSRC awards through the School of EGIS and the Institute of GeoEnergy Engineering (IGE). Once academic admission is confirmed by faculty review, top-ranked candidates are nominated directly by the supervisor (**Dr. Christos Chrysoulas**) to the school’s Doctoral Landscape Committee.
