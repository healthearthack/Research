* **Strategic Leverage:** Perfectly aligned with Andrew's target return to **The Holloway Group at UW–Madison** ([`career/The-Holloway-Group/README.md`](../career/The-Holloway-Group/README.md)).

* **Office:** Office of International Science and Engineering (OISE).
* **Purpose:** Sponsoring transatlantic scientific consortia pairing U.S. research universities with world-leading European engineering centers.
* **Application Scope:** Joint grant applications between Heriot-Watt University and the University of Wisconsin–Madison to fund high-performance reservoir simulation compute hours and student data exchange.

### C. NSF Career-Life Balance (CLB) Supplements
