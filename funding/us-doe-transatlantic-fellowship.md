## 1. Federal U.S. Funding Objectives
Under the **Inflation Reduction Act (IRA Section 45X and 30D)** and the **Energy Act of 2020**, the U.S. Department of Energy is deploying billions of dollars to secure domestic critical battery minerals (lithium) and baseload renewable power:

* **DOE Geothermal Technologies Office (GTO):** Sponsoring research on low-temperature geothermal co-production and wellbore repurposing in sedimentary basins (DOE/EE-2841).
* **ARPA-E (Advanced Research Projects Agency - Energy):** Funding high-risk, high-reward innovations in Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) from oilfield formation brines.
* **NSF International Research Networks:** Sponsoring transatlantic scientific consortia pairing U.S. institutions (UW–Madison / NASA HAQAST) with international research centers (Heriot-Watt University).

## 2. Strategic Leverage: Connecting Smackover Brines to Heriot-Watt
* **The Transatlantic Pipeline:** Andrew Kieckhefer (as an American researcher at Heriot-Watt) serves as the bridge between U.S. domestic energy operators (managing Smackover brine wells in Arkansas) and Heriot-Watt’s HPHT core flood laboratories in Edinburgh.
* **Federal Data Exchange:** Securing DOE and NSF sub-awards to fund high-performance simulation runs, satellite ground-truthing campaigns, and open-source software development.
