## 1. The Corporate Incentive: Liability to Asset Conversion
For global energy majors, plugging and abandoning (P&A) mature and idle oil and gas wells is an enormous financial liability ($150k to over $2M per wellbore). 

Corporate ventures and technology teams actively sponsor university doctoral research that demonstrates how to convert these liabilities into revenue-generating critical mineral and geothermal assets:

* **Shell Centre of Exploration Geoscience at Heriot-Watt:** Direct industry research funding through Heriot-Watt's multi-decade partnership with Shell.
* **Shell GameChanger / Ventures:** Seed-stage grants ($100,000 to $500,000) for proven computational models and digital twins that de-risk clean energy pilots.
* **SLB & Baker Hughes Subsurface Fellowships:** Co-funding PhD students who integrate fiber-optic Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and digital twin SCADA telemetry into reservoir management.

## 2. Engagement Strategy During Doctoral Tenure
* **Year 1 (Dubai):** Present initial thermodynamic simulation findings to regional energy major technology hubs in the UAE (Abu Dhabi / Dubai).
* **Year 2 (Dubai/Edinburgh):** Submit a pilot demonstration proposal to Shell GameChanger focusing on a single-well Smackover or Permian DLE test.
* **Year 3 (Edinburgh):** Leverage corporate sponsorship to subsidize specialized laboratory consumables and core flooding runs in Heriot-Watt’s HPHT facilities.
