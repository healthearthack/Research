## 1. The Human Reality: Caregiving While Executing a Doctorate
Conducting high-impact doctoral research in energy engineering should not require abandoning essential family responsibilities. For researchers serving as primary or co-caregivers for elderly parents managing chronic health conditions (e.g., congestive heart failure, cardiac arrhythmias, post-operative recovery):
* **The Challenge:** Rigid, physical on-site laboratory expectations and unpaid caregiving overhead can induce severe financial and emotional strain.
* **The Computational Solution:** This repository’s design—**cloud-accessible digital twin testbeds (`website.oil:8000` & `website.h2o:8001`) and autonomous background telemetry agents (`agent/`)**—was engineered specifically to allow tier-one simulation, modeling, and data auditing to be conducted remotely from home alongside daily family care.

## 2. Institutional & University Carer Grants (Heriot-Watt University)

### A. Heriot-Watt Athena SWAN Carer Support Fund
* **Awarding Body:** Heriot-Watt University Equality, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI) Directorate.
* **Purpose:** Provides direct financial grants to postgraduate researchers with significant family caregiving responsibilities (caring for ill, disabled, or elderly dependents).
* **Eligible Costs:**
  * Subsidizing home respite care or professional medical assistance during critical project milestones (Viva preparation, thesis submission).
  * Travel and accommodation support for dependent care during necessary academic conferences or campus visits.
  * Funding remote computational equipment and high-speed telemetry links to minimize physical lab presence.

### B. Heriot-Watt Postgraduate Hardship & Discretionary Funds
* **Awarding Body:** Student Financial Support Team, Heriot-Watt University.
* **Purpose:** Non-repayable emergency grants for enrolled students experiencing unforeseen financial hardship due to medical emergencies, dependent illness, or sudden changes in family caregiving costs.

## 3. U.S. Federal & State Caregiver Support Vehicles

### A. National Family Caregiver Support Program (NFCSP) & State Grants
* **Authorizing Statute:** Older Americans Act (Title III-E).
* **Benefits:** Provides caregiver counseling, respite care subsidies, adaptive home equipment, and supplemental emergency financial assistance for adults caring for parents aged 60+ with chronic disabling medical conditions.

### B. Supplemental Security Income (SSI) & Medicaid Caregiver Programs
* **Structured Self-Directed Care:** In many states (including Wisconsin and national programs), Medicaid self-directed waiver programs (e.g., IRIS - Include, Respect, I Self-Direct) allow adult children acting as primary caregivers for medically fragile parents to receive direct monthly caregiver stipends.
* **Tax Relief:** Federal and state Child and Dependent Care Credits, allowing deduction of unreimbursed medical and care expenses associated with dependent elderly parents.

## 4. National Science Foundation (NSF) & UKRI Dependent Accommodations

### A. NSF Career-Life Balance (CLB) Supplements
* **Agency:** National Science Foundation (NSF).
* **Mechanism:** NSF provides supplemental grant funding (up to $30,000) for researchers on NSF-supported projects who require family leave, dependent care assistance, or technical research technician support during acute family health emergencies.

### B. UKRI Paid Family & Medical Leave Provisions
* For holders of UKRI and EPSRC studentships, UKRI mandates **up to 13 weeks of paid sick/compassionate leave** and flexible part-time study transitions (e.g., 50% or 60% FTE) with pro-rated tax-free stipends to accommodate intensive caregiving without forfeiting degree candidacy.

## 5. Strategic Action Plan During Doctoral Tenure
1. **Declare Caregiver Status Upon Enrollment:** Formally notify Heriot-Watt’s Graduate School and the Athena SWAN departmental lead of primary eldercare responsibilities to secure pre-approved flexible milestone timelines.
2. **Apply for Institutional Carer Bursaries:** Submit annual requests to the Heriot-Watt Carer Support Fund to subsidize assistive monitoring hardware and medical respite care.
3. **Maintain Remote Simulation Workflow:** Ensure that all thesis deliverables, HPHT core flood simulation runs, and telemetry pipelines remain 100% executable from the local workbench ([`tools/README.md`](../tools/README.md)).
