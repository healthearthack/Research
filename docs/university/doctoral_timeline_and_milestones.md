# Doctoral Research Program Timeline & Milestone Schedule (3.5 to 4 Years)

**Candidate:** Andrew Kieckhefer  
**Target Program:** Ph.D. in GeoEnergy Engineering  
**Institute:** Institute of GeoEnergy Engineering (IGE), Heriot-Watt University  

---

## Program Phase Breakdown

---

### Year 1: Theoretical Framework & Initial Modeling (Months 1–12)
* **Q1–Q2:** Comprehensive literature synthesis on Smackover formation brine geochemistry and high-temperature DLE adsorption media.
* **Q2–Q3:** Formulation of coupled multiphase flow equations (Darcy-Brinkman transport) in mature wellbores using numerical simulation packages.
* **Q4:** **Formal Milestone:** First-Year Review & Transfer Viva (Progression from probationary candidature to full Ph.D. status).

---

### Year 2: Co-Extraction Kinetics & Reservoir Dynamics (Months 13–24)
* **Q1–Q2:** Numerical coupling of geothermal heat extraction with direct lithium adsorption kinetics.
* **Q2–Q3:** Simulation of downhole pressure transients during simultaneous multi-stream production.
* **Q4:** **Milestone:** Submission of **Journal Paper 1** (Target: *ACS ES&T Engineering* or *SPE Journal* on DLE co-production thermodynamics).

---

### Year 3: Cyber-Physical Telemetry Security & Model Validation (Months 25–36)
* **Q1–Q2:** Integration of edge-computed Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) data flows into the 360-degree risk-forecasting engine.
* **Q2–Q3:** Development of physics-aware detection algorithms to distinguish downhole pressure transients from adversarial OT network manipulation (NIST SP 800-82 / Modbus protocols).
* **Q4:** **Milestone:** Submission of **Journal Paper 2** (Target: *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics* or *Applied Energy*).

---

### Final Stage: Synthesis, Thesis Defense & Dissemination (Months 37–42)
* **Months 37–39:** Consolidation of doctoral chapters, dissertation drafting, and final supervisor review.
* **Months 40–42:** Formal thesis submission, **Oral Defense (Viva Voce)** before external and internal examiners, and award of the Ph.D. in GeoEnergy Engineering.
