# James Watt PhD Scholarship Dossier & Terms of Award

**Award Body:** Heriot-Watt University Graduate School / School of EGIS  
**Target Award:** James Watt PhD Scholarship  
**Complementary Schemes:** UKRI / EPSRC Doctoral Studentship Allocation  
**Tenure:** 3.5 to 4 Years Full-Time Postgraduate Research  

---

## 1. Award Components & Financial Structure
The James Watt PhD Scholarship represents Heriot-Watt's premier merit-based doctoral funding scheme. The award package comprises:
* **Full Tuition Fee Waiver:** 100% coverage of postgraduate research tuition fees at either the UK/Home or International/Overseas rate.
* **Annual Living Stipend:** Tax-free maintenance stipend matched to the official **UK Research and Innovation (UKRI)** minimum doctoral rate (~£19,237 to £20,500+ per annum, indexed annually).
* **Research Training Support Grant (RTSG):** Annual research allowance for field instrumentation, high-performance computing resources, and international conference dissemination.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria & Strategic Defense
Applications are evaluated on a competitive, school-wide basis by the EGIS Research Degrees Committee against three core pillars:
1. **Academic Distinction & Potential:** Proven quantitative foundation in physical and computational sciences.
2. **Novelty & Strategic Importance of the Research Proposal:** Proving a significant contribution to energy security, critical minerals (DLE), and industrial telemetry defense.
3. **Institutional Fit & Supervisor Feasibility:** Clear alignment with existing laboratory facilities and faculty expertise within the Institute of GeoEnergy Engineering (IGE).

---

## 3. Application & Compliance Audit Trail
* **Formal Portal:** Heriot-Watt University Online Application System.
* **Target Program:** Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in GeoEnergy Engineering.
* **Mandatory Declarations:** Confirmation of applicant sponsorship intent under the James Watt Scholarship scheme within the online financial questionnaire.
