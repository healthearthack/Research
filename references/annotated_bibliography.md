# Annotated Bibliography: Core Theoretical & Regulatory Pillars

This dossier details the 8 foundational citations anchoring the doctoral research proposal:  
**"Subsurface GeoEnergy Systems, Critical Minerals (DLE), & Cyber-Physical Telemetry Security"**  
Institute of GeoEnergy Engineering (IGE), Heriot-Watt University.

---

### 1. USGS & Arkansas Department of Energy and Environment (2024)
* **Citation:** *Evaluation of the Lithium Resource in the Smackover Formation Brine: Implications for Domestic Critical Mineral Independence.* USGS SIR 2024-5112.
* **Core Takeaway:** Establishes an empirical in-place reserve of **5 to 19 million tons of battery-grade lithium** dissolved in Smackover formation water.
* **Role in Thesis:** Serves as the quantitative proof that existing oilfield brine is a world-class critical mineral feedstock rather than a waste byproduct.

---

### 2. U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Geothermal Technologies Office (2024)
* **Citation:** *Co-Production of Geothermal Energy and Critical Minerals in Sedimentary and Petroleum Basins.* DOE/EE-2841.
* **Core Takeaway:** Provides thermodynamic frameworks for co-extracting high-enthalpy geothermal heat and dissolved minerals simultaneously through existing wellbores.
* **Role in Thesis:** Unlocks the second pillar of the "Trojan horse" strategy: using subsurface heat exchangers to power the DLE separation process.

---

### 3. Ground Water Protection Council (GWPC) & USGS (2024)
* **Citation:** *Continental-Scale Assessment of Produced Water Volumes, Subsurface Reinjection Dynamics, and Mineral Concentrations in Mature U.S. Basins.*
* **Core Takeaway:** Confirms that over **25 billion barrels of formation brine** are handled annually in North America alone.
* **Role in Thesis:** Demonstrates the massive scale of the disposal liability that our closed-loop engineering workflow converts into high-purity battery revenue.

---

### 4. Kumar, Vera, et al. (2024)
* **Citation:** Continuous Selective Adsorption Kinetics in Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) from High-Enthalpy Oilfield Produced Waters. *ACS ES&T Engineering*.
* **Core Takeaway:** Demonstrates high-temperature selective adsorption media capable of extracting Li+ ions from hypersaline brine in minutes without solar evaporation ponds.
* **Role in Thesis:** Provides the chemical separation kinetics governing downhole and surface processing units.

---

### 5. NIST Special Publication 800-82, Revision 3 (2024)
* **Citation:** *Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security: Physics-Aware Telemetry and Supervisory Control Systems.*
* **Core Takeaway:** Formalizes guidelines for **physics-aware operational technology (OT) monitoring**, emphasizing that cyber-attacks on industrial controllers must be detected through anomalous physical sensor behavior.
* **Role in Thesis:** Serves as the security blueprint for the 360-degree risk-forecasting engine, linking downhole pressure transients to telemetry defense.

---

### 6. CISA & Dragos Energy Threat Advisory (2024/2025)
* **Citation:** *Threat Group Targeting of Upstream Energy Infrastructure: Mitigation Strategies for Remote Wellhead RTUs, Edge Controllers, and Modbus/DNP3 Telemetry.* EA24-188.
* **Core Takeaway:** Documents active nation-state cyber targeting of legacy unauthenticated industrial protocols (Modbus, DNP3) operating wellhead chokes.
* **Role in Thesis:** Proves the urgent industrial need: operators cannot deploy multi-stream DLE and geothermal co-production on legacy, vulnerable OT networks.

---

### 7. U.S. Department of the Treasury & IRS (2024)
* **Citation:** *Final Guidance on the Clean Vehicle Credit and Advanced Manufacturing Production Credit (Sections 30D and 45X).*
* **Core Takeaway:** Mandates strict domestic extraction and processing quotas for battery critical minerals to qualify for federal clean-energy tax incentives.
* **Role in Thesis:** Establishes the macroeconomic justification: DLE co-production from domestic wellbores directly meets Section 30D/45X compliance.

---

### 8. Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) (2024)
* **Citation:** *High-Temperature Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and Downhole Edge-Computing Architecture for Real-Time Multiphase Inflow Diagnostics.* SPE-21820.
* **Core Takeaway:** Validates downhole fiber-optic Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and edge processors capable of surviving high-pressure/high-temperature (HPHT) wellbores.
* **Role in Thesis:** Provides the physical instrumentation and sensor architecture that feeds real-time downhole data into our predictive security matrix.
