## 🧭 About the `.oil` Domain
The `.oil:8001` digital twin tracks the physical integrity, geomechanics, and operational technology (OT) cybersecurity of mature petroleum wellbores repurposed for clean energy and mineral co-production. We are looking for researchers and engineers to fill three critical requisitions:

---

## 📋 Open Requisitions ("Help Wanted")

### 🛢️ Requisition OIL-01: Subsurface OT Threat & SCADA Telemetry Lead
* **Scope:** Industrial Control Systems (ICS) security for wellhead RTUs and PLCs.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Implement automated deep packet inspection for Modbus/TCP and DNP3 industrial field protocols.
  * Audit field telemetry against **NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3** and **CISA Energy Advisories**.
  * Integrate downhole Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) fiber strain metrics (SPE-21820) to detect physical barrier tampering.
* **Qualifications:** Python, C++, SCADA protocols, ICS security frameworks.

### 🛢️ Requisition OIL-02: Wellbore Geomechanics & Casing Barrier Engineer
* **Scope:** Mechanical containment and thermodynamic fatigue modeling of repurposed cased wells.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Model long-term cyclic thermal fatigue on legacy casing and cement sheaths under high-flow geothermal brine extraction ($>120^\circ\text{C}$).
  * Quantify casing burst/collapse limits to ensure annular casing pressure remains within nominal thresholds ($< 1,200\text{ psi}$).
  * Evaluate Smackover Formation carbonate rock mechanics using Heriot-Watt HPHT core flood data.
* **Qualifications:** Petroleum engineering, rock mechanics, ABAQUS/ANSYS or open-source finite element modeling.

### 🛢️ Requisition OIL-03: Satellite Methane & Fugitive Emissions Analyst
* **Scope:** Ground-truthing spaceborne spectrometer observations against wellhead telemetry.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Downscale orbital **TROPOMI** (Sentinel-5P) and **TEMPO** trace gas column data over repurposed well sites.
  * Correlate transient boundary-layer methane enhancements with surface sensor telemetry to certify **IRA Section 45X zero-leakage compliance** ($< 0.25\text{ kg/hr}$).
* **Qualifications:** Atmospheric science, NetCDF/HDF5 processing, NASA Earthdata APIs, WRF-Chem or CMAQ.
