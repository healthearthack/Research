## 📋 Open Requisitions ("Help Wanted")

### 💧 Requisition H2O-01: Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) Chemical Process Engineer
* **Scope:** Adsorption and ion-exchange resin kinetics from high-enthalpy geothermal brines.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Model continuous selective $\text{Li}^+$ adsorption kinetics based on the **Kumar et al. (2024)** framework.
  * Optimize column cycle times, wash ratios, and acid elution concentration profiles for Smackover brines ($385\text{ ppm Li}$).
  * Track column differential pressure drop ($\Delta P < 35\text{ psi}$) to detect resin fouling and bed compaction.
* **Qualifications:** Chemical engineering, separation science, geochemical modeling (PHREEQC/Geochemist's Workbench).

### 💧 Requisition H2O-02: Smart Water SCADA & Reinjection Hydraulics Lead
* **Scope:** Industrial IoT architecture and multi-well reinjection balancing under Dr. Christos Chrysoulas's Smart Water paradigm.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Design distributed telemetry control loops managing high-volume brine throughput ($14,200\text{ bpd}$).
  * Model reinjection reservoir pressure propagation ($2,100\text{ psi}$) to prevent induced seismicity and verify formation containment.
  * Implement edge anomaly detection for flow-meter drift and automated valve actuation.
* **Qualifications:** Computer science, control systems, industrial IoT, reservoir simulation.

### 💧 Requisition H2O-03: Geothermal Binary Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) Engineer
* **Scope:** Low-enthalpy surface thermodynamic heat extraction and power generation.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * Design closed-loop working fluid (isobutane/pentane) thermodynamic cycles extracting heat from $112.8^\circ\text{C}$ brine down to $64.1^\circ\text{C}$.
  * Optimize net power generation ($850\text{ kW}$ baseline / $20.4\text{ MWh/day}$) to supply parasitics for DLE facilities.
  * Balance thermal transients during wellhead flow fluctuations.
* **Qualifications:** Mechanical engineering, thermal energy systems, Aspen HYSYS or Python thermodynamic solvers.
