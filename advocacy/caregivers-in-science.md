## 1. The Systemic Crisis: The "Maternal Wall" in Laboratory STEM
Across physical sciences and engineering disciplines, the retention of mothers and family caregivers plummets sharply between the doctoral and tenured faculty stages:
* **Physical Rigidity:** Subsurface engineering, geosciences, and chemical extraction have historically demanded round-the-clock physical presence—long offshore rotations, high-pressure high-temperature (HPHT) autoclave monitoring, and inflexible weekend bench-science hours.
* **The Penalty:** Mothers and primary family caregivers are disproportionately forced to compromise their academic progression, exit the tenure track, or accept subordinate non-research appointments due to the absence of parental leave accommodations and schedule flexibility.
* **Institutional Alignment:** Heriot-Watt University is a recognized leader in advancing gender equality and work-life equity through the **Athena SWAN Charter**. This initiative actively expands that commitment into doctoral computational research.

## 2. The Technological Solution: Digital Twins as an Equalizer
The philosophy of our research repository—**"The Tools to Make the Engine"**—provides a direct, powerful technological remedy to the maternal wall:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TRADITIONAL BENCH LAB (Rigid & Inaccessible to Caregivers)             │
│ • Hazardous chemical autoclaves & high-pressure steam                   │
│ • Mandatory 24/7 on-site physical presence                              │
│ • Disproportionate attrition of mothers and primary caregivers         │
└────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                     │ Paradigm Shift: Remote Digital Twins
                                     ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ COMPUTATIONAL DIGITAL TWIN WORKBENCH (Flexible & Inclusive)             │
│ • Air-Gapped Virtual Testbeds (website.oil:8000 & website.h2o:8001)     │
│ • Cloud-Accessible Telemetry Streaming & Anomaly Detection (agent/)     │
│ • Autonomous Background Policy Scanners (scripts/junior_researcher.py)  │
│ • Full Research Autonomy: Run tier-one experiments from home on flexible│
│   schedules without sacrificing career velocity or physical safety      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Virtualizing the Physical Asset:** By building rich, accessible digital twins ([`website.oil:8000`](http://localhost:8000) and [`website.h2o:8001`](http://localhost:8001)), researchers can test complex multi-phase fluid models, calibrate Direct Lithium Extraction sorption kinetics, and audit SCADA protocols remotely from home.
2. **Automated Background Telemetry:** Autonomous agent tooling ([`run_agent.py`](../run_agent.py)) monitors simulated wellhead feeds and compiles diagnostic reports automatically, allowing researchers with caregiving responsibilities to review findings asynchronously.
3. **Safety & Health:** Eliminating unnecessary hazardous chemical exposure for pregnant or nursing researchers without stalling their publication trajectory.

## 3. Advocacy Commitments & Direct Actions

### 👶 Commitment 1: Flexible, Output-Driven Doctoral Milestones
* Championing institutional policies that evaluate postgraduate researchers based on computational outputs, publication quality, and validated models—rather than rigid physical "seat time" in laboratories.
* Advocating for remote and hybrid viva options and doctoral milestone reviews across Heriot-Watt’s Dubai and Edinburgh campuses.

### 💼 Commitment 2: Sponsoring Dependent-Care Travel & Conference Grants
* Advocating for professional scientific societies (SPE, AGU, AMS) to provide dependent-care travel grants and child-care stipends for conference presenters.
* Encouraging virtual and asynchronous poster presentations to ensure maternal researchers can maintain high international visibility without leaving their families.

### 🌐 Commitment 3: Open-Science Toolchain for Parent Researchers
* Open-sourcing all wellbore simulation tools, SCADA parsers, and policy scrapers under the MIT License, ensuring that independent researchers, student parents, and caregivers worldwide can replicate and publish peer-reviewed computational research at zero software cost.
