**Academic Background:** Project GO Arabic Language Immersion & Air Force ROTC (University of Wisconsin–Madison)  

## Core Mission
1. **Rejecting Regional Stigmas:** Countering reductive petro-state tropes in Western media by showcasing Middle Eastern leadership in clean hydrogen, utility-scale solar (MBR Solar Park), and smart grid technologies.
2. **Economic Co-Investment:** Channeling Gulf clean-energy capital and deep-reservoir expertise into American industrial basins (such as the Smackover Formation DLE co-production hub).
3. **Academic & Cultural Exchange:** Sponsoring bilateral dialogues connecting engineering students across the UAE (Heriot-Watt Dubai, Khalifa University) and the United States (UW–Madison).
